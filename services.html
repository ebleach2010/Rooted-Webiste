Lymphatic Drainage Massage

90 minutes

$140 / 90 min

One of Arielle’s specialties. Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) uses precise, rhythmic, feather-light movements that follow the body’s natural lymphatic pathways — encouraging fluid movement, easing puffiness and congestion, and producing a profound sense of calm. Unlike traditional massage, MLD uses no deep pressure at all; the unhurried 90-minute session covers you head to toe. Hydrate well before and after.

Potential Benefits

  • Encourages healthy lymphatic flow
  • May reduce mild swelling and puffiness
  • Deep nervous-system relaxation
  • A lighter, refreshed feeling all over

What to Expect

  • Extremely light, rhythmic pressure
  • Slow, precise movements along lymph pathways
  • A calming, pain-free experience
  • A full-body, head-to-toe treatment

Ideal For

  • Mild puffiness or fluid retention
  • Gentle, non-deep-pressure bodywork
  • Stress relief and relaxation
  • General wellness support
