Our Studio

A Different Kind of Massage Studio

Rooted Massage & Bodywork is a family-run massage studio serving Boise and the Treasure Valley. We opened with a simple idea: massage works best when your therapist has the time, training, and freedom to treat what’s actually going on, not rush you through a spa menu.

Rooted is the two of us. Arielle is the licensed massage therapist behind every session; Eric keeps the schedule, the books, and the lights on. We’re small by design; nothing about your care gets handed off, rushed, or templated.
