What should I expect at my first appointment?

Plan to arrive a few minutes early to go over a short health intake. Arielle will talk through what’s going on with your body and what you want out of the session, then tailor the work from there. [PLACEHOLDER: adjust to your actual intake flow.]

Do I have to undress?

Only to your comfort level. You’ll always be professionally draped, and great work can be done through clothing if you prefer. Your comfort runs the session.

Should a massage be painful?

A massage should never be more intense than you want it to be. Deeper work can involve some “good hurt” as stubborn knots release, but pressure is always adjusted to you — just say the word at any point during your session.

Is there talking (or not talking) during my treatment?

Entirely up to you. Many clients prefer quiet; others like to chat. Arielle follows your lead — the only thing she’ll always check in about is pressure and comfort.

Can I add hot stones, aromatherapy, or cupping?

Yes — add-ons run $5–$35: aromatherapy ($5), hot stones or a peppermint scalp massage ($10), cupping or fascial scraping ($15), and a specialized warm vibration scraping tool ($35). You choose them while booking, right after picking your massage. Note that Deep Tissue and Lymphatic Drainage already include their specialty treatments free, and Relax & Reset includes free aromatherapy.

Do you have a new-client special?

Yes! First-time clients get 15% off a Signature Custom Massage plus one free add-on — use code WELCOME15 when booking online. One use per client, Signature massages only.
